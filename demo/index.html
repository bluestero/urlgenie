generalize(url) — cleans and standardizes a single URL. Automatically recognizes and canonicalizes social profile links (Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, YouTube) as well as ordinary websites.

Advanced flags (keep_path, keep_userinfo, force_https)
facebook.com/#!/AhmedKhatib..90 → https://www.facebook.com/ahmedkhatib90
platform: facebook
handle: ahmedkhatib90
original handle: AhmedKhatib..90
rule matched: fb_fragment_handle
Recognized as a social profile — periods stripped and case folded, since Facebook ignores both when resolving a profile URL.

generalize_many(urls) — cleans a batch of URLs at once, one per line or comma-separated. Every result stays aligned with its input, even the invalid ones.

Keep query / keep fragment / lowercase / keep path / keep userinfo / force HTTPS — same flags as the Generalize tab, applied to every row.
